      Sample QR codes
      
  
      All fictional. Suspicious samples use reserved test domains (.example, example.com) and a TEST-NET IP address, so they cannot reach any real site. Tap “Scan this sample” to decode the image through the same pipeline as an upload.
      
  
      

      


      
  
      How it works & limitations
  
      Decoding: jsQR reads the QR image locally on a canvas. Camera frames are processed in memory only.
      
  
      Scoring: each heuristic adds points: shorteners (+3), raw IP (+4), user@host tricks (+3), punycode/non-ASCII (+3), brand lookalikes (+3), plain HTTP (+2), unusual port (+2), 3+ subdomains (+2), redirect parameters (+2), executable downloads (+3), suspicious keywords (+1 each, max 3), long URL (+1/+2), risky TLD (+1). Score 0–1 = Safe, 2–4 = Caution, 5+ = Suspicious.
      
  
      • Heuristics only: there is no reputation or blocklist lookup, and short links are not expanded (that would require visiting them).
        • 
  
      • “Safe” means no red flags found, not guaranteed safe. A compromised legitimate site will look fine.
        • 
  
      • Lookalike detection covers a small built-in brand list and simple typo/leet patterns.
        • 
  
      • Camera scanning needs HTTPS and permission; some embedded previews block the camera, so open the page in its own tab.
        • 
  
      • Damaged, tiny or low-contrast QR images may fail to decode.
